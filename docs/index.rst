How to Contact Big Fish Games Customer Service Support
=======================================================

If you need help with a Big Fish Games account, game, purchase, billing issue, or technical problem, you can contact Big Fish Games Customer Service through its official support system.

Contact Customer Support
------------------------

Big Fish Games provides customer support through its online Help Center. You can search for solutions to common problems or submit a support request if you need additional assistance.

The support team can help with issues such as:

* Account and sign-in problems
* Game installation and technical issues
* Billing and purchasing questions
* Missing purchases
* Game crashes or errors
* Refund-related questions
* Big Fish Game Club concerns
* Other game-related problems

Submit a Support Request
------------------------

If you cannot find a solution in the Help Center, submit a customer support request and describe your problem clearly.

After submitting your request, you should receive a confirmation message. A customer support representative will then respond to the email address provided with your request.

Big Fish Games currently states that the first response to support requests generally takes around two to three business days.

Information to Include
----------------------

When contacting customer service, provide enough information to help the support team understand and investigate your issue.

Useful details may include:

* Your account email address
* The name of the affected game
* A description of the problem
* Any error messages
* Your device or operating system
* Relevant purchase information
* Troubleshooting steps you have already tried
* Screenshots, when helpful

For account or billing issues, provide only the information requested by customer support. Do not send your password or complete payment-card information.

Check Your Email
----------------

After submitting a support request, check the email inbox associated with your request. If you do not see a response, check your spam or junk-mail folder.

If you need to provide additional information about an existing request, reply to the existing support email rather than submitting multiple requests for the same problem.

Account and Login Issues
------------------------

If you are having difficulty signing in, first try the available password-reset and account-recovery options.

For older Big Fish Games accounts, additional assistance may be necessary. In this situation, provide details about the account and explain when you last successfully accessed it.

Technical Support
-----------------

For technical problems, describe exactly what happens when you try to start or play the game.

Include information such as:

* The game title
* Your device or operating system
* The error message
* When the problem started
* Whether the problem occurs every time
* Troubleshooting steps already attempted

Conclusion
----------

Big Fish Games customer service is available through its online support system. Start by searching the Help Center for an answer. If you cannot resolve the issue, submit a support request with clear information about your account, game, purchase, or technical problem.

Providing complete details and responding to the existing support conversation can help the customer service team investigate the issue more efficiently.
